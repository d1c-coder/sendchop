How it works

Four stops from your phone to their door

  1. Your phone

    Choose who it's for, pick a meal from a kitchen near them, write a message and pay by card or transfer.

  2. Their phone

    They get a text with a link. No app. They confirm the address and the time that suits them.

  3. A kitchen near them

    A local partner kitchen cooks the order and packs it with your message.

  4. Their door

    A rider brings it. The recipient shares a delivery code, and you know it arrived.

    You're paid once the delivery is confirmed with the recipient's code.
